# CHROMABOUND Player Manual

Open `index.html` through any static web server and press **Enter** or **Start / Continue**.

## Controls
- Move: **Arrow keys** or **WASD**
- Attack: **Space** or **Z**
- Attune Prism Blade: **1 Crimson**, **2 Verdant**, **3 Azure**
- Dash: **Shift** after defeating the Ashen Warden
- Quick potion: **Q**
- Inventory / pause: **I** or **Esc**
- Mute: **M**
- Secret console: **`** backtick

Touch controls appear on mobile/coarse-pointer devices.

## Hue Attunement
The world is drained of color. Your blade can attune to three hues:
- **Crimson** burns thorn walls and hurts crimson enemies hardest.
- **Verdant** lets you pass living vine gates and punishes verdant enemies.
- **Azure** solidifies blue bridges and punishes azure enemies.

Matching an enemy hue deals double damage; mismatching deals half.

## Potions
- **Health Potion** restores hearts.
- **Chroma Tonic** grants temporary all-hue attunement.
- **Stone Skin** reduces incoming damage.

The Shardmason Bazaar south of the hub sells potions and permanent upgrades.

## Hints
Two bosses can be challenged in either order. Defeating them restores color to their regions and unlocks new power. One wall hides a prism vault. Some secrets answer players who type old legends, colors, wealth, or warps; the full list is in `DEVLOG.md`.
